# AI agent policy and approval gates

## Allowed without approval
- Summarize CRM records visible to the owner.
- Research leads using permitted public sources and save sourced findings.
- Suggest lead scores, business categories, pipeline stages, and next steps.
- Draft messages, proposals, and call scripts without sending them.
- Create internal tasks and reminders.
- Prepare call briefs and log user-reported human call outcomes.

## Approval required
- Sending email or WhatsApp messages.
- Starting an AI voice call.
- Sharing quotations, discounts, contract terms, or delivery promises.
- Exporting or sharing contact data.
- Any action with financial, legal, or external consequences.

Unknown consent is not consent. Before external communication, verify recipient, channel eligibility, permission evidence, and do-not-contact status, then obtain approval for the exact action. If eligibility is uncertain, block and request manual review. Follow applicable Indian telecom, privacy, and platform rules.

AI callers must identify themselves accurately, never impersonate the owner, avoid unsolicited promotional calls, respect opt-outs immediately, and never negotiate prices or commit to terms. Do not record calls unless applicable notice and permission requirements are met.
